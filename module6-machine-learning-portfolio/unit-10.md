**Artefact:** Evaluating speech-to-text on technical language ([presentation transcript](../projects/unit-11-individual-presentation/Presentation_Transcript.docx))

My Unit 11 transcript was produced with Whisper, a Transformer-based speech recognition model (Radford et al., 2023). It handled everyday speech well but misheard technical terms, turning "scikit-learn" into "psychic learn" and "ReLU" into "Rolleu", and garbling author names. Each error had to be corrected by hand.

**What I learned:** an NLP model's accuracy depends on its domain. A low overall error rate can hide systematic errors on exactly the specialist words that matter most. A transcript submitted for assessment therefore has to be checked by a person.
